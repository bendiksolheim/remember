Always one keystroke away.

Remember is a simple todo app for your Mac. Press ⌥ ⌘ Space from anywhere, type the task, hit Return, and you're back to what you were doing.

This is the real thing, running in your browser. Start typing.

Download for Mac macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon Or install with Homebrew

Always there

Remember never sits more than one keystroke away, and it's ready for a new task the moment it appears. No loading, no window to find, no thinking about where things go.

Your tasks live on your Mac and work offline. Sync between your devices is there if you want it.

Only what fits

Lists, tasks, and due dates when you need them. That's most of it, and that's on purpose.

A feature only gets in if it fits with everything else and has a real role in the app. You get fewer features, and each one is well thought out.

Clear, and all keyboard

The interface is easy to understand at a glance, and you can do everything from the keyboard.

Install with Homebrew

brew tap bendiksolheim/tap https://github.com/bendiksolheim/remember
brew install --cask remember

Update with brew update && brew upgrade --cask remember. More details, and the source code, are on GitHub.
